By using Volyr you agree to this User Agreement. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

What Volyr provides

Volyr lets you keep an on-device tasting archive for durian: cultivar, whole and flesh weights, yield, five sensory scores, photos, voice notes, and saved tasting cards you can compare across a season. Volyr does not sell fruit, broker stalls, or host a public social feed.

Your responsibilities

Only capture photos and notes you have the right to keep. Do not use the app for unlawful, deceptive, or infringing content.

Credits

Saving a finished tasting card uses Credits after you confirm. Drafts, previews, edits, cancelled saves, and failed retries do not charge again. Credits are consumable digital items sold through Apple In-App Purchase. They have no cash value outside Volyr. A new install or a full local reset may receive an opening balance of 3 Credits that is not an App Store product.

Accounts

Access uses a one-tap local session. Log Out ends the session and keeps tasting cards on this device. Delete Account permanently removes the local session and associated on-device data, including remaining Credits. A later one-tap login starts a new local account.

Local data

Clear All Data permanently deletes tasting cards, drafts, media, preferences, and remaining Credits on this device and returns you to first-run setup with a new opening balance of 3 Credits. Uninstalling the app also removes sandbox data.
