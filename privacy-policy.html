Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

Volyr is an on-device durian tasting archive. You log cultivar, weight, flesh yield, five sensory scores, photos, and voice notes on this iPhone. Volyr does not operate a cloud account backend in this version.

Data stored on this device

Tasting cards, drafts, photos, voice notes, speech transcripts used as tasting verdicts, cultivar notes, export files, a local one-tap session identifier, and a consumable Credits ledger are stored in the app sandbox (on-device JSON and the app’s private media folder). We do not upload this content to a Volyr server.

One-tap session

Sign-in is a local one-tap session on this device. There is no Sign in with Apple and no username or password. Log Out ends the session and keeps your tasting cards on this device. Delete Account removes the local session identifier and the data that session wrote on this device, including remaining Credits.

Permissions

Camera is requested only after you tap Take Photo or Add Photo. Photo library read is requested only after you tap Import from Photos. Photo library add is requested only after you tap Save to Photos. Microphone and speech recognition are requested only after you tap Record Voice; audio and recognition stay on this device for tasting notes. After you reach the main interface, the system notification prompt may appear so Volyr can show alerts, sounds, and a badge for tasting or archive reminders on this device; you can change this in iOS Settings. Volyr does not request App Tracking Transparency or collect advertising identifiers for ads or cross-app tracking.

Purchases

A new install or a full local reset may receive an opening balance of 3 Credits on this device. That opening balance is not an App Store product and is not restored by Apple. Additional Credits are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. Apple charges your App Store account. Volyr grants purchased Credits only after a verified StoreKit transaction and records the transaction identifier so the same purchase is not granted twice. Saving one tasting card uses 1 Credit after you confirm. Consumable Credits stay on this device and cannot be restored after you delete the app, clear app data, or delete the account. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple.

Sharing

Volyr does not sell tasting cards or create a public feed. Export and share-sheet actions stay under your control on this device.
